# Privacy Policy for HisabPro – Monthly Expense Tracker
**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 28, 2026  

HisabPro ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal financial data. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the HisabPro mobile application.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Processing

**HisabPro does not collect, transmit, store on remote servers, or share any of your personal financial records.**

* **Local On-Device Storage:** All financial records—including transactions, income, expense logs, budget targets, bank account balances, savings goals, and notes—are stored exclusively within your device's local storage sandbox.
* **No Server-Side Database:** HisabPro does not operate external databases that collect or synchronize user information.
* **No Account Creation:** You do not need to provide an email address, phone number, password, or login credentials to use the application.

---

### 2. Device Permissions Used

HisabPro requires minimal permissions to operate:
* **Internet Permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required exclusively by the Android WebView runtime to render local client-side assets and interface components over the internal `https://localhost` scheme. The app does not send your personal financial entries across the internet.
* **No Sensitive Permissions:** HisabPro **never** requests access to your contacts, SMS messages, phone records, location/GPS, camera, microphone, or external files without explicit user action.

---

### 3. Backup, Restore, and Data Portability

* **User Controlled Exports:** You have full control over your financial data. You may export your data at any time via:
  * **JSON Backup:** A complete snapshot of your data for migration or backup.
  * **CSV Spreadsheet Export:** Transaction history readable by Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet tool.
* **Storage Location:** When you export a backup or CSV, the file is saved to the destination chosen by you on your device or cloud storage. We have no access to these exported files.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Deletion

* Because data is stored locally on your device, it remains available as long as you keep the app installed.
* **Immediate Deletion:** You can delete all your financial records at any time by navigating to **Settings > Reset & Cleanup > Clear All Transactions**, or by clearing the app's data in Android System Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

* HisabPro contains **no third-party advertising SDKs** (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or Unity Ads).
* HisabPro contains **no behavioral tracking or analytics SDKs** (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or AppsFlyer).

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

HisabPro does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published within the repository and application.
